**Note for the owner:** the Learn project does not say where each of its pictures and sounds
originally came from (one of its pictures, not used here, is marked as coming from Freepik). If anyone
asks for a file to be removed, delete it from `media/` and remove that animal from the list near the
top of `js/courses.js` (`FARM`, `WILD` or `BIRDS`).

Files used: animals: bear, cat, cow, deer, dog, elephant, fox, giraffe, goat, horse, kangaroo, lion,
monkey, rabbit, sheep, snake, squirrel, tiger, zebra. Birds: bulbul, crow, duck, eagle, goose, hen,
hummingbird, kingfisher, ostrich, owl, parrot, pigeon, robin, seagull, sparrow, swan, vulture,
woodpecker.
